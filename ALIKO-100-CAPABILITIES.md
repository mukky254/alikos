## Next production-grade upgrades
The next leap beyond this MVP is not another 100 buttons. It is better underlying geospatial intelligence: road map matching, traffic/incident data, offline map packs, background navigation, lane-level guidance where data exists, destination entrance coordinates, pedestrian routing for the final 50–100 metres, observability, rate limiting, caching, queueing, abuse detection, and native Android/iOS navigation.
